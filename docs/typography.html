Typography, previews and kerning

A typeface is more than a set of drawings. Hieroglyph shows the letters working as text while you edit, so spacing problems surface immediately.

  1. The preview dock
  2. Text, alphabet and kerning tabs

The preview dock

The bottom of the window renders your developing typeface with the same outlines the editor is using — no compilation step and no installed font required. It updates as you move a point.

  • Sample text — type anything. The font's own sample sentence is used when the field is empty.
  • Size — from 12 to 180 pixels.
  • Tracking — extra space between every pair, from −80 to 200 thousandths of an em.
  • Leading — line spacing from 0.8 to 2.4 times the size.
  • Ink and ground — switch between dark ink on paper and light ink on charcoal.
  • Collapse — the chevron hides the dock; the toolbar button beside the style switcher brings it back.

Text, alphabet and kerning tabs

Text

Wraps your sample through the dock at the chosen size, tracking and leading, in the same colours as the final specimen. Blank characters leave a visible gap, which makes missing glyphs easy to spot.

The specimen viewer

View → Specimen Viewer… presents the typeface the way a designer would show it: a large Aa, both alphabets, digits, punctuation, a paragraph and the metrics.

The sample-text field at the top of the specimen overrides the family sample, and the header's Export button opens the export dialog without leaving the sheet.

The kerning editor

Kerning is the adjustment of space between specific pairs. Open Kerning in the inspector on the right.

ControlWhat it does
Pair previewShows the two characters at a large size with the current adjustment applied and the advance gap marked.
Value fieldThe adjustment in font units. Negative pulls the pair together, positive pushes it apart.
− / + buttonsNudge the pair by five units for quick judgement.
Suggested pairsAV, VA, WA, AW, To, Ta, Yo and LT, the pairs most typefaces need first.
Pair listEvery pair in the style. Click a row to preview it, or remove it with the ×.
Add a pairType the two characters and a value to create any pair.
Kerning ships with the font Exported fonts include both a legacy kern table and an OpenType GPOS pair-positioning lookup, so the spacing works in desktop software and on the web.
Start with the diagonals Capitals with diagonal strokes — A, V, W, Y — look too loose without kerning. Fix those six or eight pairs and the typeface immediately reads better.
